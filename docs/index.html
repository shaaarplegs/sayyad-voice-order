Voice ordering · pilot

Say it like you would at the harbour.

Buyers ask for fish and fishermen offer their catch, just by speaking. Sayyad writes down what was said, understands it, and prepares the order.

Tap to speak Speak in Arabic or English
  1. Voice received
  2. Writing down what was said
  3. Understanding the request
  4. Ready

Two ways to use it

For buyers

Order fish

Say which fish you want and how much.

“أبغي ١٠٠ كيلو كنعد و٥٠ كيلو شعري”

“I’d like 100 kg of kingfish and 50 kg of emperor.”

For fishermen & suppliers

Offer your catch

Say which fish you have, how much, and your price per kilo.

“عندي ٢٠٠ كيلو تونة و٨٠ كيلو هامور، الكيلو بريالين”

“I have 200 kg of tuna and 80 kg of grouper, 2 rials a kilo.”

Sayyad voice ordering is a pilot for review. Recordings are processed by OpenAI (speech to text) and DeepSeek (understanding). Nothing is ordered for real.
