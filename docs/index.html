Voice ordering · pilot

Say it like you would at the harbour.

Buyers order fish and sellers issue invoices, just by speaking. Sayyad writes down what was said, works out the details, and asks for anything missing.

Two ways to use it

Missed a detail? Record just the missing part; the draft stays open until it is complete.

For buyers

Order fish

Say the fish, how much, and where to deliver.

“أبغي ١٠٠ كيلو كنعد و٥٠ كيلو شعري، التوصيل للعامرات”

“I’d like 100 kg of kingfish and 50 kg of emperor, delivered to Al Amerat.”

For sellers

Create an invoice

Say what you sold, how much, and the price per kilo.

“بعت لأحمد ٢٠٠ كيلو تونة بريالين الكيلو و٨٠ كيلو هامور بثلاث ريالات”

“I sold Ahmed 200 kg of tuna at 2 rials a kilo and 80 kg of grouper at 3 rials.”

Sayyad voice ordering is a pilot for review. Recordings are processed by OpenAI (speech to text) and DeepSeek (understanding). Nothing is ordered for real.
